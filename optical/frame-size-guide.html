The five ranges are a general frame-width guide. They do not replace a professional fitting. A frame’s lens width, bridge width and temple length are separate model-specific measurements.

Understand the numbers printed on a frame

53–18–140

Example only: 53 mm lens width · 18 mm bridge width · 140 mm temple length. These are three different measurements and are not the overall frame-width bands above.

Lens width is measured horizontally across one lens. Bridge width is the distance between the lenses. Temple length follows the arm from hinge to tip.

How to estimate your fit at home

  1. Stand in front of a mirror with a standard bank or credit card.
  2. Hold one short edge at the centre of your nose, with the card extending toward the outer corner of your eye.
  3. Use the card position only as a rough indication of frame size.
  4. For a more reliable fit, ask an optical professional to measure your face and adjust the frame.

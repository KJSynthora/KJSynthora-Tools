Frame size guide.

Use this guide as a starting point. The best fit depends on the frame design and your face measurements.

Five general frame-width bands

Extra Narrow

126–129 mm overall frame width

Narrow

130–133 mm overall frame width

Medium

134–137 mm overall frame width

Wide

138–141 mm overall frame width

Extra Wide

142 mm and above

These are general reference bands, not a fit guarantee. Frame shape, bridge design, lens size and individual facial measurements all matter.

Understand the numbers on a frame

53–18–140

Example only: 53 mm lens width · 18 mm bridge width · 140 mm temple length. Check the inside of an existing frame's arm for its size code.

Lens width is measured horizontally across one lens. Bridge width is the distance between the lenses. Temple length follows the arm from hinge to tip.

How to estimate your fit at home

  1. Stand in front of a mirror with a standard bank or credit card.
  2. Hold one short edge at the centre of your nose, with the card extending toward the outer corner of your eye.
  3. Observe whether the card edge ends before, around, or beyond the outer eye corner. This offers only a rough size indication.
  4. For a more reliable fit, ask an optical professional to measure your face and adjust the frame.

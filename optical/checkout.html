What happens next?

  1. Your order details open in WhatsApp.
  2. The store confirms real product availability and final pricing.
  3. Delivery or pickup options are discussed.
  4. Payment method is confirmed directly with the store.

Do not send card numbers, CVV, passwords or OTPs in WhatsApp.

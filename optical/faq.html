Frequently asked questions.

Quick answers before you visit or enquire.

Where is KNR Optical located?

1st Shop, Eluru Road (Main Road, Corner), Chinthastop, Opp. Happy Mobile's, Near Ramamandiram, Arundalpet, Governor Peta, Vijayawada, Andhra Pradesh 520002. Open Maps.

What are your opening hours?

Daily, 10:00 AM to 10:00 PM. Please call to confirm on holidays.

Can I check product availability on WhatsApp?

Yes. Send the product name or a photograph and ask the store to confirm availability and final price.

Can I book an eye test?

Submit an appointment enquiry. The store must confirm service availability and your appointment.

Can I order online?

You can submit an order enquiry through the website checkout and continue via WhatsApp. Online card payment is not enabled in this starter package.

Are all brands shown on the website available in store?

No. The brand directory is a suggested discovery list. Please contact KNR Optical to confirm brands and models actually stocked.

Do you accept wholesale enquiries?

Use the wholesale enquiry form to share your business and quantity requirements.

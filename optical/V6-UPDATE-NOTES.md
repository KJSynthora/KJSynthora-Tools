# KNR Optical V6 – mobile refresh and campaign update

## Changes
- Reduced mobile utility/header/announcement height and corrected the oversized logo override.
- Kept large editorial headings on desktop while scaling them responsively on phones.
- Reworked mobile hero spacing, CTA layout, carousel controls and caption placement.

## Video needed to complete the video experience
Please provide either:
1. KNR Optical's own MP4 video file (ideally 1080p, 16:9 or 9:16), or
2. A YouTube/Vimeo URL that KNR Optical owns or is authorized to embed.

The current film panel is a branded visual preview only. It must be connected to an approved KNR video before it is described as a playable campaign film.

## Measurement policy
The frame-width preference bands are:
- Extra Narrow: 126–129 mm
- Narrow: 130–133 mm
- Medium: 134–137 mm
- Wide: 138–141 mm
- Extra Wide: 142 mm+

These are general fit bands, not model-specific measurements. The product's lens width, bridge width and temple length must come from the actual frame or verified inventory record.

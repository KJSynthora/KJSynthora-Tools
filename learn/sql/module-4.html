Subqueries & CTEs

Subqueries and CTEs (Common Table Expressions) let you break complex multi-step problems into clean, readable pieces. By the end of this module you'll confidently write subqueries in WHERE and SELECT, write correlated subqueries that reference the outer query row-by-row, structure multi-step logic with WITH clauses (including recursive CTEs), and know exactly when to reach for EXISTS, IN, or a JOIN — and why it matters for performance.

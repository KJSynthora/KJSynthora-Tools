What is KJSynthora?

KJSynthora is a free online productivity platform offering 90+ tools across six categories — PDF, AI, Image, Finance, Utility and Developer tools. Built by an independent developer, every tool on this platform is designed to run entirely in your browser with zero signup, zero installation and zero cost.

Whether you need to merge PDF files, build an AI-powered resume, remove an image background or calculate your home loan EMI — KJSynthora has it all in one place, instantly available without any account.

Why KJSynthora Was Built

Most productivity tools online either require expensive subscriptions, demand unnecessary signups, or force you to upload sensitive files to remote servers. KJSynthora was created to solve all three problems — every tool is free, no account is ever required, and files are processed locally in your browser so your data never leaves your device.

AI Resume Builder — Land More Interviews

The KJSynthora AI Resume Builder uses Groq's ultra-fast AI inference to generate ATS-optimized resumes tailored to specific job roles. Unlike generic templates, this tool creates unique resume content based on your actual experience and target job.

Getting your resume past an Applicant Tracking System (ATS) is the first challenge in any job search. Studies show that over 75% of resumes are rejected by ATS before a human ever reads them. Our AI analyzes the job requirements and crafts your resume with the right keywords, structure and formatting to pass ATS screening and impress hiring managers.

What the Resume Builder Does

  • Generates professional resume content using AI based on your input
  • Optimizes for ATS keyword matching and formatting requirements
  • Supports multiple resume formats — chronological, functional, combination
  • Creates tailored summary, skills and experience sections
  • Allows instant PDF download — no account needed
  • Works for all industries: IT, healthcare, finance, marketing, engineering

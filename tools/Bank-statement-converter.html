Frequently Asked Questions

Which banks are supported?
We support 50+ Indian and international banks including SBI, HDFC, ICICI, Axis, Kotak, PNB, BOI, Canara, Federal, Yes Bank, IDFC, IndusInd, RBL, UCO, IOB, Bank of Baroda, Union Bank, Punjab & Sind, Bandhan Bank, DCB, Karnataka Bank, South Indian Bank, Nainital Bank, and major international banks like Citibank, Standard Chartered, HSBC, DBS, and more. The Auto-Detect mode works for any bank not explicitly listed.
Is my bank statement data safe?
Yes, 100%. All file processing happens entirely in your browser using JavaScript. Your bank statement is never uploaded to our servers, never stored, and never transmitted anywhere. Once you close the tab, everything is gone.
Can it convert scanned/image bank statements?
Yes — JPG, PNG and WebP scanned statements are supported. The tool uses browser-based OCR-like processing to extract transaction tables from images. For best results, use a clear, high-resolution scan.
What does Auto-Categorize do?
Auto-Categorize reads each transaction description and assigns it a category like Food & Dining, Shopping, Utilities, UPI Transfer, ATM Withdrawal, Salary Credit, EMI/Loan, Entertainment, Medical, and more. This makes expense analysis much faster.
What output formats are available?
You can download the converted statement as Excel (.xlsx), CSV (.csv), JSON (.json), or a formatted PDF summary report. Excel files include multiple sheets: Transactions, Summary, and Category Analysis.

❓ FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about editing PDFs online.

Is this PDF editor completely free? ▼
Yes. KJSynthora PDF Editor is 100% free with no signup, no watermark, no file limits, and no hidden costs. You can edit and download unlimited PDFs.
Is my PDF file uploaded to a server? ▼
No. All PDF processing happens entirely in your browser using PDF.js and Fabric.js. Your files never leave your device. This makes it safe for confidential documents, contracts, medical records, and sensitive business files.
How do I add text to a PDF? ▼
Type your text in the Content field in the left panel, choose font size and color, then click Add Text. The text box appears on the current page — drag it to position. Double-click the text to edit it inline.
How do I add a digital signature to a PDF? ▼
Click the ✍️ Sign button in the Annotation Tools section. A signature pad appears — draw your signature with your mouse or touchscreen. Click Add to PDF to embed it on the current page. Resize and position as needed, then download.
Can I highlight text in a PDF? ▼
Yes. Click the 🟡 Highlight button in Annotation Tools, then draw a rectangle over the text you want to highlight. Choose from yellow, green, red, or blue highlight colors. The highlight is semi-transparent so the text remains visible.
How do I redact (cover) sensitive information? ▼
Use the White Cover button in the Shapes section. A white rectangle is placed on the page — drag and resize it to cover the sensitive text or information. Download the PDF to permanently hide the covered content.
Does it support multi-page PDFs? ▼
Yes. All pages of a multi-page PDF are loaded and displayed. Use the Prev/Next Page buttons to navigate. You can add text, annotations, and shapes on any page independently. All pages are included when you download the final PDF.

🧮 How EMI is Calculated
EMI = P × r × (1+r)ⁿ / [(1+r)ⁿ − 1]
Where: P = Principal Loan Amount  |  r = Monthly Interest Rate (Annual Rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100)  |  n = Total Months (Years × 12)
01
Monthly Rate
Annual interest rate is divided by 12 to get the monthly rate. Example: 8.4% → 8.4/12/100 = 0.007
02
Compound Factor
The formula accounts for compound interest — each month's outstanding balance generates interest on the reducing principal.
03
Amortization
Early EMIs have more interest, later ones have more principal. Total EMI stays fixed throughout the loan tenure.
⚠️ Estimates are illustrative only. Actual rates depend on underwriting, credit policies, and central bank benchmarks. Tax benefits apply only in India for Home Loan. Consult a certified financial advisor before making loan decisions.

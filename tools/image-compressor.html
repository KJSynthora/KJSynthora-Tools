Frequently Asked Questions

Does compressing an image reduce its quality?
It depends on the mode. "Balanced" mode reduces file size by 60-80% with minimal visible quality loss. "Lossless" compresses without any quality loss. Use the quality slider and visual comparison to find your perfect balance.
Is the image compressor really free?
Yes, completely free with no signup, no account, no watermark, and no file count limits. You can compress unlimited images for free.
Are my images safe and private?
100% safe. Your images are never uploaded to any server. All compression happens locally in your browser using JavaScript. No one can access your images — not even us.
What image formats are supported?
Supports JPG/JPEG, PNG, WebP, GIF, BMP, and TIFF. You can also convert any format to WebP for the best web performance — WebP images are typically 25-35% smaller than JPG at the same quality.
Can I compress multiple images at once?
Yes! Upload any number of images and compress them all in one click. Download individually or as a ZIP file containing all compressed images.
What is WebP and why should I use it?
WebP is a modern image format by Google that provides superior compression. WebP images are typically 25-34% smaller than JPEG and 26% smaller than PNG at equivalent visual quality. All major browsers support WebP in 2026.

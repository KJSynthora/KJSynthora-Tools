Frequently Asked Questions

How do I crop a PDF page online?

Upload your PDF, enter the crop margins (top, bottom, left, right) in millimeters, then click "Crop & Download". Your cropped PDF is ready instantly.

Can I remove white margins from a PDF?

Yes. Use the "Remove Margins" preset or manually enter the margin values to trim any unwanted white space from your PDF pages.

Does cropping a PDF reduce file size?

Cropping adjusts the visible page area but may not reduce file size significantly. Use our PDF Compress tool for size reduction.

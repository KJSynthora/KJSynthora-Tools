📚 Learn

Understanding Song Structure

🎼
Verse
Tells the story — narrative section that changes lyrics each time but keeps the same melody.
🎵
Chorus
The most memorable, repeated part — carries the main hook and emotional core of the song.
🌉
Bridge
A contrasting section, usually appearing once, that builds tension before the final chorus.
🎤
Pre-Chorus
A short transitional section that builds energy and anticipation leading into the chorus.
🔁
Hook
A short, catchy, repeated phrase or line — common in pop and rap for instant memorability.
✍️
Rhyme Scheme
The pattern of rhymes at line endings — AABB, ABAB, ABCB — shapes the song's rhythm and flow.

Genre Guide

🎤
Pop
Catchy hooks, simple relatable lyrics, repetitive choruses. Universal themes — love, life, fun.
💕
Romantic
Emotional, poetic imagery focused on love, longing, and connection between two people.
🎙️
Rap/HipHop
Rhythmic wordplay, internal rhymes, storytelling or braggadocio, strong hooks.
🪕
Folk
Storytelling tradition, often about nature, life lessons, community, and heritage.
🎸
Rock
Bold, emotional, often rebellious themes with powerful choruses and anthemic energy.
🙏
Devotional
Spiritual themes — gratitude, faith, prayer — often with repetitive, meditative structure.

📖 How It Works

How to Remove Background from Image

Follow these simple steps to remove any image background for free — no software, no signup needed.

Step 01
📤
Upload Your Image
Drag & drop or click to upload your JPG, PNG, or WEBP image. Up to 20MB supported. Your file stays local — never uploaded to any server.
Step 02
🎛️
Choose Removal Mode
Select Person AI for portraits and people photos. Use Color Key for logos, product shots, green screens, and solid-color backgrounds.
Step 03
⚙️
Adjust Settings
Set AI quality, segmentation threshold, edge feathering, and choose your output background style — transparent, gradient, custom color, or custom image.
Step 04
🪄
Remove Background
Click "Remove Background". TensorFlow BodyPix AI analyzes the image and removes the background in seconds, right inside your browser.
Step 05
⬇️
Download Your Result
Download as transparent PNG, JPG, or WEBP. Or copy directly to clipboard. Use Re-apply Background to try different backgrounds without re-processing.

❓ FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our free online background remover.

Is this background remover completely free? ▼
Yes. KJSynthora Background Remover is 100% free with no signup, no watermark, no download limits, and no hidden costs. All processing runs in your browser using TensorFlow.js.
Does it upload my images to a server? ▼
No. All processing happens entirely in your browser using TensorFlow.js and HTML5 Canvas API. Your images never leave your device — not even for a second. This also means it can work offline after the model has loaded once.
What is the difference between Person AI and Color Key mode? ▼
Person AI uses TensorFlow BodyPix deep learning to detect and segment people in photos — ideal for portraits, headshots, and human subjects with complex edges like hair. Color Key mode removes a specific solid background color (like green screen, white studio background, or logo backgrounds) and works on any image type, not just people.
How do I remove a white background from a logo? ▼
Switch to Color Key mode, upload your logo, then click directly on the white area of the image to automatically pick that color. Adjust the Tolerance slider to capture more or less of similar shades, then click Remove Background. Download as PNG to preserve transparency.
What image formats are supported for input and output? ▼
Input: JPG, PNG, and WEBP images up to 20MB are supported. Output: You can download results as PNG (with transparency/alpha channel), JPG (white background composited), or WEBP. You can also copy the result directly to your clipboard.
Can I add a custom background after removing? ▼
Yes. After removing the background, use the Output Background selector to choose from transparent, white, black, gradient presets (blue-purple, sunset, green, dark space), a custom solid color, a blurred version of the original, or upload your own custom background image. Use Re-apply Background to switch backgrounds without re-running AI.
Why is the AI model slow to load? ▼
The TensorFlow BodyPix model is approximately 5-8MB and loads from a CDN on first use. After it loads once, it may be cached by your browser for faster subsequent loads. During loading, Color Key mode is immediately available as a fast alternative.
What does Edge Feathering do? ▼
Edge Feathering softens the edges of the cutout by gradually fading transparency near the boundary between subject and background. A feathering value of 0 gives sharp, hard edges. Higher values (4-8) create smoother, more natural-looking edges that blend better with custom backgrounds.

❓ FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I find the best hashtags for Instagram in 2025?
Enter your niche or topic, select Instagram, and click Generate. The AI creates a mix of high-volume, mid-range and niche hashtags. For best results, use a 30/30/40 split — 30% popular tags, 30% medium tags, 40% niche. Our tool does this automatically based on your niche mix slider.
What is a shadowban and how do I avoid it?
Instagram shadowbanning means your posts are hidden from hashtag pages and Explore without any notification. It happens from using banned, broken or massively overused hashtags. Our AI checks each generated tag against known risk patterns and gives you a risk rating. Always aim for Low Risk.
How many hashtags should I use on Instagram, TikTok and LinkedIn?
Instagram: 10-20 for Reels, 5-15 for feed posts (max 30 allowed). TikTok: 3-5 relevant hashtags plus 1-2 trending tags. LinkedIn: 3-5 professional hashtags only — more looks spammy. Twitter/X: 1-2 hashtags maximum per tweet. Our tool generates platform-specific recommendations.
Is this AI hashtag generator free?
Yes, completely free. You need a free Groq API key from console.groq.com — no credit card required. The key is stored only in your browser's localStorage, never sent to our servers. Without an API key, the tool uses smart template-based generation as a fallback.
What is the difference between high-volume and niche hashtags?
High-volume hashtags (#fitness, #marketing) have millions of posts — you get buried quickly but reach a huge audience. Niche hashtags (#kettlebellcoach, #b2bcontentmarketing) have fewer posts but your content stays visible longer and reaches a more targeted, engaged audience. The ideal strategy combines both.
Can I use the same hashtags for every post?
No — Instagram and TikTok penalize copy-paste repetitive hashtag use as spam behavior. Rotate 3-5 different hashtag sets for similar content. Use our History feature to save different sets and rotate them across your posts.
Is my topic and API key private?
Yes. Your topic inputs, generated hashtags and API key are never sent to KJSynthora's servers. API calls go directly from your browser to Groq. Your history is saved only in browser localStorage and can be cleared anytime.

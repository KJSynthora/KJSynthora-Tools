How to Split a PDF Online for Free — Complete Guide 2025

PDF splitting is one of the most common document tasks — separating a report into chapters, extracting invoices from a bulk PDF, or creating handouts from a presentation. This guide explains every split mode and when to use each.

Which Split Mode Should You Use?

  • Every Page: Creates one PDF per page. Use when you need to email individual pages or convert a multi-page scan into separate files.
  • Page Range: Extracts a continuous range (e.g. pages 5–12) into one file. Use for extracting a chapter, section or report segment.
  • Every N Pages: Splits into equal-sized chunks. Use for dividing a 100-page document into groups of 10 for distribution.
  • Custom Pages: Select any pages in any order (e.g. 1, 3, 5-8, 12). Use for extracting non-contiguous pages or creating a custom subset.
  • Odd/Even Split: Creates two PDFs — odd pages and even pages. Use for duplex printing, scanning reversal, or interleaving documents.
💡 Privacy note: All PDF splitting happens entirely in your browser using JavaScript. Your file never leaves your device and is never uploaded to any server. This makes it safe for confidential documents, contracts, and medical records.

Odd/Even Split — Practical Uses

The odd/even split is particularly useful for duplex (double-sided) printing with a single-sided printer. Print the odd pages, flip the stack, then print even pages. The two PDFs from this tool are ready for exactly that workflow.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I split a PDF online for free? ▼
Upload your PDF, choose a split mode, then click Split PDF. Files download instantly — no signup needed.
Is my PDF safe? Does it get uploaded to a server? ▼
No. All PDF processing happens in your browser using JavaScript. Your file never leaves your device and is never uploaded to any server. This makes it safe for confidential documents.
Can I extract specific non-contiguous pages? ▼
Yes. Use Custom Pages mode and enter page numbers with commas and dashes — for example: 1, 3, 5-8, 12. This extracts exactly those pages merged into one PDF.
What is odd/even page split used for? ▼
The odd/even split creates two PDFs — one with pages 1,3,5… and one with 2,4,6… It's useful for duplex printing on single-sided printers, or when merging two scanned stacks.
Can I download all split files at once? ▼
Yes. After splitting, a "Download All" button appears that downloads every output file. For single-output splits (page range, custom, odd/even), the file downloads automatically.

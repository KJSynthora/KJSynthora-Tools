Frequently Asked Questions

How does the word counter work?
Type or paste your text into the editor. The tool automatically counts words, characters, sentences, and paragraphs in real-time as you type. No button click needed. It also calculates reading time, speaking time, keyword density and readability score.
How is reading time calculated?
Reading time is based on 200 words per minute (average adult reading speed). Speaking time is based on 130 words per minute. These are averages — actual times vary by content complexity and individual reader speed.
Can I upload a Word document to count words?
Yes! Click the Upload button to upload a .txt or .docx (Microsoft Word) file. Text is extracted instantly and analyzed. All processing happens locally in your browser — no file is uploaded to any server.
What is keyword density and why does it matter for SEO?
Keyword density is how often a word appears as a percentage of total words. For SEO, aim for 1-3% for primary keywords. Too low means the keyword may not rank well; too high can be seen as keyword stuffing by search engines like Google.
Is my text saved or sent anywhere?
Your text is only saved to your browser's localStorage as an autosave draft. It is never sent to any server. All analysis happens locally in your browser using JavaScript. Clearing browser storage removes the draft.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this online calculator completely free?
Yes, 100% free with no signup, no account, and no limits. All 10 calculator modes are completely free forever.
How does the EMI calculator work?
The EMI calculator uses the standard reducing balance formula: EMI = P × r × (1+r)ⁿ / ((1+r)ⁿ − 1), where P is principal, r is monthly interest rate, and n is number of months. It also supports flat rate EMI.
What scientific functions are supported?
sin, cos, tan (and inverses), sinh, cosh, log, log₂, ln, exp, sqrt, cbrt, factorial, modulo, floor, ceiling, absolute value, and constants π and e. DEG/RAD switching supported.
How does the programmer calculator work?
Shows BIN/OCT/DEC/HEX simultaneously. Supports bitwise AND, OR, XOR, NOT, SHL (shift left), SHR (shift right). Click any base row to copy that value.
Can I use keyboard shortcuts?
Yes! Type digits, operators, and press Enter to calculate, Backspace to delete, Escape to clear. Press ? to see the full shortcut list.

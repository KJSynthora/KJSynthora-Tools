Frequently Asked Questions

Is this online calculator completely free?
Yes, 100% free with no signup, no account, and no limits. All 8 calculator modes — Standard, Scientific, Finance, Unit Converter, Programmer, Statistics, Matrix, and Graph — are completely free.
How does the EMI calculator work?
The EMI calculator uses the standard reducing balance formula: EMI = P × r × (1+r)ⁿ / ((1+r)ⁿ − 1), where P is principal, r is monthly interest rate, and n is number of months. It also supports flat rate EMI calculation.
What functions does the scientific calculator support?
It supports sin, cos, tan (and inverses), sinh, cosh, log, log₂, ln, exp, sqrt, cbrt, factorial, modulo, floor, ceiling, absolute value, and constants π and e. You can switch between DEG and RAD modes.
How does the programmer calculator work?
The programmer calculator lets you work in BIN (base 2), OCT (base 8), DEC (base 10), and HEX (base 16). It shows all representations simultaneously and supports bitwise operations: AND, OR, XOR, NOT, SHL (shift left), SHR (shift right).
Can I plot multiple functions on the graph?
Yes! You can enter two functions — f(x) and g(x) — and both will be plotted simultaneously in different colors. You can also adjust the X-axis range to zoom in or out.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Word to PDF converter free?
Yes, completely free. All features — Word to PDF, PDF to Word, Split, Merge, Compress, Password Protect — are free with no signup, no account, and no file limits.
Is my document safe? Does it get uploaded?
Your files are never uploaded to any server. All conversion — Word to PDF, PDF splitting, merging — happens entirely in your browser using JavaScript libraries (jsPDF, mammoth.js, PDF.js). Your documents stay 100% private on your device.
How do I convert Word to PDF?
Upload your DOCX file using the dropzone. Choose page size, orientation and font size options. Click "Word → PDF" button. The PDF downloads instantly to your computer. No waiting, no server processing.
Can I merge multiple PDFs?
Yes! Click the Merge tab, upload multiple PDF files. Drag the file items to reorder them, then click Merge. The combined PDF downloads instantly.
How much can I compress a PDF?
Depending on the content, you can reduce PDF size by 10% (Low), 35% (Medium), 55% (High), or up to 72% (Extreme). Image-heavy PDFs compress much more than text-only PDFs.

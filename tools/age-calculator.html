How to Calculate Your Exact Age in Years, Months and Days

Most people know their age in years — but do you know your exact age in months, days, or even seconds? Here's how precise age calculation works and why it matters.

To calculate your exact age, subtract your date of birth from today's date. The result gives you years, remaining months, and remaining days. For example, if you were born on January 15, 1995 and today is June 5, 2026 — your exact age is 31 years, 4 months, and 21 days.

Why months and days matter: For legal documents, insurance calculations, and medical records, your age in complete years may not be precise enough. A child's vaccination schedule, for instance, is based on exact months of age.

The formula: Years = floor(days/365.25). Remaining months = floor((days % 365.25)/30.44). This tool handles all edge cases including leap years automatically.

🌍 International

Korean Age vs International Age — What's the Difference?

In South Korea, everyone is born at age 1 — not 0. And everyone turns a year older on January 1st, not their birthday. Here's how Korean age works.

Korean Age (세는나이): You are 1 year old at birth. Everyone ages 1 year on January 1st regardless of their actual birthday. This means a child born on December 31st would be 2 years old (Korean age) the next day.

International Age: You start at 0 and age 1 year exactly on each birthday. This is the standard used in most countries including India, USA, UK, and Europe.

Lunar Age: Used in traditional Chinese culture, based on the lunar calendar. Your age advances on Chinese New Year rather than your birthday.

What Is Your 10,000th Day Alive? (And Why It's Worth Celebrating)

Your 10,000th day alive happens around age 27.4 years. It's one of the most unique milestones nobody talks about — and it's worth knowing exactly when yours is.

10,000 days works out to approximately 27 years, 4 months, and 18 days. This milestone has been gaining attention as a meaningful "coming of age" checkpoint that doesn't rely on arbitrary year numbers.

Other day milestones to celebrate: Your 1,000th day (~2.7 years), 5,000th day (~13.7 years), and 1 billion seconds (~31.7 years) are all worth marking on your calendar.

Understanding Biorhythm Cycles and How They Affect Your Day

Biorhythm theory suggests your physical, emotional and intellectual performance follow predictable 23, 28 and 33-day cycles from the day you were born.

Physical cycle (23 days): Governs your strength, energy, coordination and overall physical well-being. High points are good for athletics and physical work.

Emotional cycle (28 days): Influences mood, creativity, sensitivity and emotional stability. Near the peak you feel positive; near the trough, more introspective.

Intellectual cycle (33 days): Affects analytical thinking, logical reasoning and memory. Schedule complex tasks and important decisions during high intellectual phases.

How to Calculate Retirement Age and Plan Your Working Years

Your retirement year isn't just a number — it's the sum of thousands of working days. Here's how to calculate exactly how many working days, months and years you have left.

In India, the standard retirement age for government employees is 60, while private sector employees often plan for 58-65. Use this calculator to see exactly how many years, months, and working days remain until your target retirement age.

Working days calculation: Assuming 260 working days per year (52 weeks × 5 days), multiply by your remaining years. At 30 years to retirement, that's 7,800 working days — more concrete than an abstract year count.

Zodiac Signs by Birthday — Which Sign Are You?

Your zodiac sign is determined by your date of birth. From fiery Aries to intuitive Pisces, each of the 12 signs carries unique personality traits and elemental associations.

Western astrology divides the year into 12 signs based on the sun's position at birth. Each sign spans roughly 30 days and is associated with one of four elements: Fire (Aries, Leo, Sagittarius), Earth (Taurus, Virgo, Capricorn), Air (Gemini, Libra, Aquarius), and Water (Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces).

Chinese Zodiac: Based on a 12-year cycle, each year associated with an animal. The cycle repeats: Rat, Ox, Tiger, Rabbit, Dragon, Snake, Horse, Goat, Monkey, Rooster, Dog, Pig.

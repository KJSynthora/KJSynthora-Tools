Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert JPG to PNG for free? ▼
Upload your JPG file above, select "PNG (Lossless)" from the Output Format dropdown, click "Process Image", then click "Download Result". No signup, no watermark, completely free — processing happens in your browser.
Will converting JPG to PNG lose quality? ▼
No. PNG is a lossless format, meaning every pixel is preserved exactly. However, the JPG already experienced quality loss when it was first saved. Converting to PNG won't recover that, but it won't add any new loss either.
Does this tool upload my images to a server? ▼
No. KJSynthora processes all images entirely in your browser using the HTML5 Canvas API. Your images never leave your device. This also means it works offline after the page loads.
What is the maximum file size I can convert? ▼
Since processing is browser-based, the limit depends on your device's RAM. Most modern devices handle images up to 50–100MB without issues. Very large RAW files may slow down the browser.
Can I batch convert multiple images at once? ▼
Yes! Click the "Batch" tab, upload up to 50 images at once, choose your output format and quality, and click "Process All". Each file is processed and available for download individually or all at once.
What's the difference between WEBP and PNG? ▼
WEBP is a modern format that supports both lossy and lossless compression, plus transparency. It typically produces files 25–34% smaller than PNG with the same quality. PNG has universal browser support; WEBP is supported in all modern browsers (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge).

📖 Guide

How to Use the AI Grammar Checker

KJSynthora's AI Grammar Checker uses Claude AI to analyze your text for grammar, spelling, punctuation, and style errors — then provides specific corrections with explanations. Switch between Grammar Check, Paraphrase, Rewrite, Tone Change, and Summarize modes for different writing needs.

STEP 01
Paste Your Text
Type or paste any text into the editor. Click Sample to try with example text containing common errors.
STEP 02
Choose Mode
Select Grammar Check, Paraphrase, Rewrite, Tone Changer, or Summarize from the mode tabs above.
STEP 03
Analyze
Click Check Grammar. Claude AI analyzes the text and returns specific suggestions with explanations.
STEP 04
Review Suggestions
Each suggestion shows the original error, the fix, and why it's wrong. Accept or dismiss individually.
STEP 05
Copy & Use
Click Accept All to apply all corrections, then Copy or Download the corrected text.

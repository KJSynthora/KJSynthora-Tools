Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Excel to PDF converter completely free?
Yes, 100% free. No signup, no account, no payment. All conversion features including multi-sheet, custom themes, and download are completely free with no limits.
Is my Excel file safe and private?
Absolutely. Your Excel file is never uploaded to any server. All processing — reading, converting, and generating the PDF — happens entirely within your browser using JavaScript. Once you close the tab, everything is gone.
What Excel formats are supported?
We support XLSX (Excel 2007 and newer), XLS (older Excel format), and CSV files. Both small and large spreadsheets are handled well.
Can I convert multiple sheets to one PDF?
Yes! When you upload an Excel file with multiple sheets, you can either select a specific sheet to convert or choose "All Sheets" to merge all sheets into a single PDF document, each starting on a new page.
What page sizes and orientations are available?
You can choose A4, A3, Letter, or Legal page sizes. Both Portrait and Landscape orientations are available. Landscape is recommended for wide spreadsheets.
Will there be a watermark on the PDF?
No watermarks. The generated PDF is completely clean and professional — ready to share or print. Only a subtle footer "kjsynthora.com" is added, which you can disable in options.

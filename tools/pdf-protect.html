❓ FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about PDF password protection.

Is this PDF protection tool completely free? ▼
Yes. KJSynthora PDF Protect is 100% free with no signup, no watermark, and no usage limits. Protect unlimited PDFs without paying anything.
Is my PDF file uploaded to a server? ▼
No. All encryption happens entirely in your browser using pdf-lib.js. Your PDF and passwords never leave your device and are never sent to any server. This makes it safe for confidential documents.
What is the difference between user and owner password? ▼
The user password (document open password) is required to open and view the PDF. The owner password controls permissions — with it, you can change or remove restrictions. Without it, the permission restrictions apply. If you leave the owner password blank, the user password is used for both.
Can I restrict printing or copying on my PDF? ▼
Yes. Use the Permissions section to toggle printing, text copying, editing, and annotation access. These restrictions are enforced by compatible PDF readers like Adobe Acrobat and Chrome's built-in PDF viewer.
What happens if I forget the password? ▼
There is no way to recover a forgotten PDF password without specialized tools. Make sure to save your password in a secure password manager. We do not store your password — only you have it.
Which PDF readers support the protection applied here? ▼
The encryption applied is standard RC4/AES and is supported by Adobe Acrobat, Adobe Reader, Google Chrome PDF viewer, Microsoft Edge, Foxit Reader, and virtually all modern PDF applications.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the AI bio generator completely free?
Yes — template-based generation is completely free with no limits. For AI-powered (Groq) generation, you need a free API key from console.groq.com — no credit card required. The Groq free tier is very generous.
What platforms does it support?
LinkedIn (220 chars), Instagram (150 chars), Twitter/X (160 chars), GitHub (160 chars), Founder/Portfolio (500+ chars), and YouTube channel description. Each platform has specific character limits and style optimization built in.
What is an ATS-optimized bio?
An ATS (Applicant Tracking System) optimized bio includes relevant industry keywords, clear job titles, and measurable achievements. Our AI Bio Generator automatically embeds high-search keywords based on your skills and industry.
Can I edit the generated bios?
Yes! Every generated bio is fully editable directly in the tool. The character counter updates in real-time as you type, and the live preview updates instantly.
Is my data safe?
100% safe. Your profile information is processed locally in your browser. Your Groq API key is stored only in your browser's localStorage and never transmitted to our servers. AI requests go directly from your browser to Groq's API.
How many bios does it generate?
For each platform, it generates 4 different variant styles: Authority Bio, Creator Bio, Minimalist Bio, and Hook Bio. With 6 platforms, that's up to 24 unique bios in one click!

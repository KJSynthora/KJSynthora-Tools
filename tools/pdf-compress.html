Frequently Asked Questions

How much can I compress a PDF? ▼
Results vary by content. PDFs with many high-resolution images can be reduced by 50–90%. Text-only PDFs typically reduce by 10–30%. Scanned PDFs reduce by 20–60%. Use Screen preset for maximum compression.
Does compressing a PDF reduce its quality? ▼
The Screen preset reduces image resolution — fine for web viewing but not professional printing. The Printer (300 DPI) and Prepress (max quality) presets maintain visual quality. For text-only PDFs, all presets preserve 100% quality.
Is my PDF uploaded to any server? ▼
Never. All processing happens in your browser using pdf-lib and PDF.js — open source JavaScript libraries. Your file never leaves your device. This means it works offline too, after the page initially loads.
Can I compress password-protected PDFs? ▼
Yes, if you know the password. Use the Protect tab to unlock the PDF first (enter the current password), then compress the unlocked version. If you don't know the password, the file cannot be processed.
What's the difference between merging and appending PDFs? ▼
Merging combines multiple PDFs into one document while preserving all pages in order. Appending adds pages to an existing PDF. Our Merge tool lets you drag to reorder PDFs before combining them.
Can I extract specific pages from a PDF? ▼
Yes. Use the Split tab and select "Page Range" mode. Enter ranges like "1-3, 5, 8-10" to extract exactly those pages as a new PDF. You can also split every page into individual files or split into equal N-page chunks.

About KJSynthora PPT to PDF

A free, private browser-based PowerPoint to PDF converter. No server upload, no tracking, no ads. Built with jsPDF and JSZip — your files stay on your device.

🔒

Zero Data Exposure

PPTX files are read entirely locally. No file bytes are sent over the network. You can verify this via browser DevTools → Network tab.

🎨

6 Themed Outputs

Professional dark, light, blue, green, minimal, and sunset PDF themes. Each produces clean, branded output suitable for sharing.

🔍

Slide Content Preview

JSZip extracts and parses the PPTX XML to show you titles and bullet points before converting. Confirm your file was read correctly.

💧

Watermark Support

Add custom watermark text with configurable opacity and font size. Useful for CONFIDENTIAL, DRAFT, or branded document distribution.

📑

Slide Range Control

Convert only specific slides from a large presentation. Set From and To slide numbers to extract exactly what you need.

📋

Table of Contents

Optionally include an auto-generated table of contents page listing all slide titles with slide numbers.

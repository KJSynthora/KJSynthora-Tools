Frequently Asked Questions

Which Indian banks are fully supported? +
We support 50+ banks: SBI, HDFC, ICICI, Axis, Kotak, PNB, BOI, Bank of Baroda, Canara, Union Bank, UCO, IOB, Federal, Yes Bank, IDFC First, IndusInd, RBL, Bandhan, DCB, Karnataka Bank, South Indian Bank, J&K Bank, AU Small Finance, Equitas, Ujjivan, Paytm Payments Bank, Airtel Payments Bank, and 20+ international banks. Auto-Detect works for any unlisted bank.
Is my bank statement data secure? +
100% secure. All processing happens in your browser using PDF.js and SheetJS — your file never leaves your device. You can verify in browser DevTools Network tab — zero upload requests.
Why does it show 0 transactions for my PDF? +
This happens if: (1) Your PDF is scanned/image-based — convert to JPG and upload instead. (2) The PDF is password-protected — remove password first. (3) The bank format is unusual — try selecting your specific bank from the list. (4) Non-standard date format — try uploading CSV downloaded from net banking instead of PDF.
How does PDF bank statement conversion work? +
The tool uses PDF.js to extract text page by page, groups text items by Y-axis position to reconstruct lines, then applies date and amount pattern matching to identify transaction rows. Multiple fallback strategies are tried automatically. For scanned PDFs, use high-resolution JPG or PNG.
What output formats are available? +
Six export formats: Excel .xlsx (multiple sheets), CSV, JSON, PDF summary report, Monthly Excel (each month as separate sheet), and Filtered CSV (exports only current view). All include categories and cleaned descriptions.
Can I process multiple files at once? +
Yes — select multiple files and all are parsed and merged into one output. Useful for combining 3–6 months or multiple accounts. Duplicate removal is automatic when enabled.

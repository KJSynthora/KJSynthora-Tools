❓ FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate the number of days between two dates?
Enter a start date and end date. The calculator instantly shows the exact difference in days, weeks, months, years, hours, minutes and business days. It handles leap years and month-length differences automatically.
What are business days and how are they counted?
Business days are working days — Monday through Friday, excluding weekends. Our calculator counts every weekday between your two dates. Enable "Exclude Holidays (India)" to also skip Indian public holidays like Republic Day, Independence Day, Gandhi Jayanti and Diwali.
Can I calculate my exact age in days?
Yes! Click the "My Age" preset button — it sets your from date to your birthdate (you can edit it) and today as the to date. The result shows your exact age in years, months, days, hours and even seconds.
How many days until New Year / Christmas?
Click the "New Year" or "Christmas" preset button. The calculator instantly sets today as the start and your chosen event as the end, showing the exact countdown in days, hours and minutes.
What is the difference between calendar days and business days?
Calendar days count every single day including weekends and holidays. Business days only count Monday–Friday working days. A 7 calendar day period might only contain 5 business days if it includes a weekend.
How do I share my date calculation with someone?
Click the "Share" button after calculating. It copies a URL with your dates pre-filled that you can send to anyone. They'll see the exact same calculation when they open it.
Is my data private?
Yes. All calculations happen entirely in your browser using JavaScript. No dates or results are sent to any server. Your calculation history is saved only in your browser's localStorage.

Frequently Asked Questions

How to convert JPG to PDF free online?
Upload your JPG images using the dropzone, arrange them in order using drag-and-drop, select page size and orientation, then click "Images → PDF". Your PDF downloads instantly — no signup, no watermark.
Can I convert multiple images to one PDF?
Yes! Upload multiple JPG, PNG, or WebP images. They will be combined into a single PDF in the order you arrange them. Use the drag handles to reorder, or the Up/Down buttons in the toolbar.
Are my images safe and private?
100% safe. Your images are never uploaded to any server. All processing — conversion, OCR, merging — happens entirely in your browser using JavaScript. No one can access your files.
What is OCR and how to extract text from images?
OCR (Optical Character Recognition) reads text from images. Upload a photo of a document, click "Run AI OCR", and the tool extracts all readable text using Tesseract.js. Supports 50+ languages including Hindi, Telugu, Arabic, Chinese, Japanese.
Can I merge multiple PDFs?
Yes! Upload multiple PDF files and click "Merge PDFs" to combine them into one document. All pages are preserved in the original order.
What image formats are supported?
Supports JPG/JPEG, PNG, WebP, BMP, GIF, and TIFF. You can also upload PDF files for PDF-to-images extraction or PDF merging.

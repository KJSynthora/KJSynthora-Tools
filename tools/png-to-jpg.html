Why Convert PNG to JPG?

📦
Reduce File Size
A PNG photo can be 5-10× larger than the JPG equivalent at 85% quality. Huge savings for web use.
📱
Social Media
Instagram, Facebook, Twitter prefer JPG for photo uploads. PNG may be rejected or auto-converted.
⚡
Faster Page Load
Smaller JPG images load faster on websites, improving Core Web Vitals and SEO rankings.
📧
Email Attachments
Email providers often limit attachment size. JPG photos are much smaller than PNG versions.
🖨️
Printing Services
Many print labs accept JPG only. Convert PNG screenshots or designs before ordering prints.
💾
Storage Savings
Bulk-converting a photo library from PNG to JPG can free up 60-80% of storage space.

Frequently Asked Questions

JPG files are significantly smaller than PNG for photographs and complex images. A PNG photo can be 5-10× larger than the equivalent JPG at 85% quality. JPG is also more widely supported by social media platforms, email clients, and print services. Converting reduces storage usage and speeds up website loading times.

JPG uses lossy compression, so some image data is mathematically discarded. At 80-90% quality, the difference is invisible to the human eye for photographs. For images with sharp edges, solid colors, text, or logos, PNG retains better quality — do not convert those. For photographic images, JPG at 85% is the sweet spot: visually lossless but much smaller.

JPG does not support transparency (alpha channel). When converting a PNG with a transparent background, the transparent areas must be filled with a solid color. White is the default, which works for most use cases. Use the background color picker to choose black, gray, or any custom color to match your use case.

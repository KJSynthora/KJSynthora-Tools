❓ FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about unit conversion.

How many kilometers in a mile? ▼
1 mile = 1.60934 kilometers. Conversely, 1 kilometer = 0.621371 miles. The conversion factor is based on the international definition of the mile as exactly 1.609344 km.
How to convert Celsius to Fahrenheit? ▼
The formula is: °F = (°C × 9/5) + 32. For example, 0°C = 32°F (freezing), 100°C = 212°F (boiling), and 37°C = 98.6°F (human body temperature). To convert Fahrenheit to Celsius: °C = (°F − 32) × 5/9.
How many grams in an ounce? ▼
1 ounce (oz) = 28.3495 grams. 1 pound = 16 ounces = 453.592 grams = 0.453592 kg. These conversions are useful for cooking, shipping, and fitness tracking.
How many MB in a GB? ▼
1 GB (Gigabyte) = 1024 MB (Megabytes) in binary (IEC standard), or 1000 MB in decimal (SI standard). Storage manufacturers typically use decimal (1000), while operating systems usually display binary (1024), which is why a 1TB drive shows as ~931 GB in Windows.
How many feet in a meter? ▼
1 meter = 3.28084 feet. 1 foot = 0.3048 meters. 1 inch = 2.54 centimeters. These exact values are defined by the international yard and pound agreement of 1959.
What is the difference between mass and weight? ▼
Mass is the amount of matter in an object, measured in kilograms (kg). Weight is the gravitational force on that mass, measured in Newtons (N). In everyday usage, "weight" (kg, pounds) typically refers to mass. On the Moon, your mass stays the same but your weight (N) is about 1/6th of Earth's.
